*Simpl-Labs is an environment for trying out Simpl-Open without having to set everything up yourself. There you can prototype a data space, and test whether your own solutions work together with Simpl-Open.*

---

### Two purposes

Simpl-Labs has two purposes ([the Simpl programme](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/dashboard/simpl-labs)):

- **Sandbox:** create and manage data spaces for testing, with wizards and reusable templates. This suits initiatives that are at an early stage or in planning.
- **Interoperability test environment:** deploy your own components and run conformance tests against Simpl-Open. The tests are based on the European Commission's Interoperability Test Bed (ITB).

Simpl-Labs is therefore not only for those who want to adopt Simpl-Open. It is also an arena for testing whether solutions built in other ways can work together with the European setup.

---

### Status

- The feasibility study is complete, with a requirements specification, architecture, a working prototype and a report.
- The implementation phase started in February 2026 ([Ana Juan Ferrer (European Commission), March 2026](https://coe-dsc.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Ana-Ferer-180326_Simpl_DataSharingFestival.pdf)). In March 2026, a first version (MVP) was announced for autumn 2026.
- In October 2026, DSSC referred to Simpl-Labs for interoperability testing as something coming in 2027 ([DSSC](https://dssc.eu/articles/from-guidance-to-deployment-how-simpl-helps-put-dsscs-guidance-into-practice)). When the service will be generally available is therefore unclear.
- The code is openly available at [code.europa.eu/simpl/simpl-labs](https://code.europa.eu/simpl/simpl-labs). The documentation site for Labs has regular releases (v0.4.0 came on 13 November 2026).

---

### The user group (User Interest Group)

Simpl-Labs has set up a user group (User Interest Group, UIG) where data space initiatives and other interested parties give input on what Labs should offer. The group holds its kick-off meeting in Brussels on 9 December 2026, the same day as the Simpl Hackathon and the day before the Simpl Annual Community Event. Digdir and the Institute for Energy Technology (IFE) are represented in the user group.

For Norwegian actors, the user group is an opportunity to influence Labs while the service is still being shaped, and to get early access to a common European test environment.

---

### What Norwegian actors can use Simpl-Labs for

*This is our assessment.*

- Test whether Norwegian shared national solutions and sector solutions can work together with European data spaces, for example for identity, access control and data catalogues.
- Prototype Norwegian or Nordic data spaces before choosing technology.
- Try out cross-sector data sharing at an unclassified level, including between civilian and military actors in total defence. See [Relevance for Norway](../relevans-for-norge/).

In Norway, the Institute for Energy Technology (IFE) also runs a sandbox for testing data space infrastructure solutions, including Simpl. It may be a natural place to start for practical testing.

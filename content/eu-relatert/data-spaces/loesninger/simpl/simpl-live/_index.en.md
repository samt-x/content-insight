*Simpl-Live is the support for specific European data spaces that are to adopt Simpl-Open. Each initiative first gets a feasibility study with a tailored roadmap, and then help putting Simpl-Open into operation.*

---

### Wave 1 and wave 2

Simpl-Live takes in data spaces in waves.

| Wave | Data spaces and initiatives | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Wave 1 | Public procurement (PPDS), secondary use of health data (EHDS2), language (LDS), European Open Science Cloud (EOSC), Destination Earth (DestinE), Smart Communities (SCDS) | The feasibility studies were completed in 2024. Implementation started in February 2026 |
| Wave 2 | Energy (CEEDS), agriculture (CEADS), mobility (EMDS), Green Deal (GDDS), the EuroCloud initiative | Feasibility studies from 2025. The second study, with EuroCloud and ten member states, was being finalised in March 2026. The conclusions were published on 5 June 2026 ([document library](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/topic/simpl-live-document-library)) |

Sources: [Simpl-Live](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/programme/simpl-live), [Ana Juan Ferrer (European Commission), March 2026](https://coe-dsc.nl/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Ana-Ferer-180326_Simpl_DataSharingFestival.pdf), [consolidated feasibility study report for wave 1](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2025-06/Simpl-Live-Feasibility-Study-Report-%28Consolidated%29_Simpl_20250328_V1.2%28A%29.pdf).

### Status of wave 1 by initiative

The programme divides implementation into five steps: review of requirements and scenarios, technical validation, acceptance, operational deployment and maintenance ([the Simpl programme, August 2026](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2026-08/Simpl_Live_Implementation_Phase_Visual.pdf)). The milestone report for wave 1 shows how far each initiative has come.

| Initiative | Review | Technical validation | Acceptance | Operational deployment | Maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Language (LDS) | Done | Ongoing | Not started | Not started | Not started |
| Public procurement (PPDS) | Ongoing | Not started | Not started | Not started | Not started |
| European Open Science Cloud (EOSC) | Ongoing | Not started | N/A | N/A | N/A |
| Destination Earth (DestinE) | Ongoing | Not started | Not started | N/A | N/A |
| Smart Communities (SCDS) | Ongoing | Not started | Not started | Not started | Not started |
| Secondary use of health data (EHDS2) | Ongoing | Not started | Not started | Not started | Not started |

Source: [Simpl-Live wave 1, milestone report](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/programme/simpl-live/wave-1), last updated 24 August 2026. The status as of November 2026 may have moved on.

As of August 2026, then, none of the wave 1 initiatives had reached acceptance or operational deployment.

Later summaries from the programme give some indications. In July 2026, the integration between the language data space (LDS) and Simpl-Open was under way as a proof of concept, through a gateway that does not interfere with LDS's own operation ([Coffee with Simpl 3](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/news/coffee-simpl-3rd-session-recap)). In October, the programme was working on integrating Simpl-Open with EOSC ([Coffee with Simpl 4](https://simpl-programme.ec.europa.eu/news/coffee-simpl-4th-edition-recap)).

### Testing outside Simpl-Live

Some are adopting Simpl-Open on their own, outside Simpl-Live. Two examples come from the pilots in DS4SSCC, which is building the data space for smart communities:

- **Helsinki:** Forum Virium Helsinki set up Simpl-Open (version 3.0.4) in a cloud service from January 2026, in the Traffic Flow Data Space pilot. On 11 June 2026 the whole flow for onboarding new participants worked, from application to approval. The repository also documents many problems along the way ([the pilot's timeline on GitHub](https://github.com/ForumViriumHelsinki/traffic_flow_data_space/blob/main/doc/simpl-eval-cloud/overview/deployment-timeline.md)).
- **Valencia:** The NEXCITY pilot (Valencia and Rotterdam, August–October 2026) states that Valencia "operates a live SIMPL node" ([DS4SSCC, NEXCITY](https://www.ds4sscc.eu/nexcity)). *This is the pilot's own statement, and we have not been able to verify it.*

There may be more such examples that we do not know of.

### What it means for Norwegian actors

*This is our assessment.* Most Norwegian actors will probably not come into contact with Simpl because they have chosen it themselves, but because their sector does. When, for example, health, procurement, mobility or energy build on Simpl-Open, Norwegian participants in these data spaces must be able to connect. See [Description of each data space](../../../beskrivelse-av-hvert-data-space/) and [Norwegian participation](../../../norsk-deltakelse/) for the individual sectors.
